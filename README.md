# ROI-Lens: Startup Idea Validator & Investor Matching Platform

A comprehensive SaaS platform that helps aspiring entrepreneurs validate their business ideas and connect with relevant investors using AI and data-driven analytics.

## 🚀 Features

### For Entrepreneurs (Idea Validation)
- **AI Idea Analysis**: Instant feedback on idea uniqueness, market fit, and scalability
- **Financial Projection**: Automated revenue forecasting and cost estimation
- **Competitor Research**: Real-time analysis of similar businesses and market trends
- **Investor Matching**: Personalized recommendations of investors looking for similar ventures
- **Document Generation**: Professional pitch deck and business plan creation

### For Investors (Deal Sourcing)
- **Portfolio Dashboard**: Track investment opportunities and portfolio performance
- **Smart Recommendations**: AI-powered matching with vetted startups
- **Due Diligence Reports**: Automated background checks and risk assessment
- **Trend Analysis**: Identify emerging sectors and investment hotspots
- **Network Management**: Connect with co-investors and industry experts

## 🎯 Problem Solved

### For Entrepreneurs
- **High Failure Rate**: 90% of startups fail due to poor market fit
- **Funding Difficulty**: 80% of entrepreneurs struggle to find investors
- **Lack of Feedback**: No instant validation for early-stage ideas

### For Investors
- **Deal Flow Overload**: Sifting through hundreds of pitches is time-consuming
- **Information Gap**: Difficulty assessing early-stage startup credibility
- **Missed Opportunities**: Important ventures may go unnoticed

## 💡 How It Works

### 1. Idea Submission
Entrepreneur enters business idea with basic details

### 2. AI Validation
- Uniqueness analysis using NLP on 100M+ databases
- Market trend analysis using Google Trends & social listening
- Financial projection with machine learning models

### 3. Investor Matching
Algorithm matches idea with investors based on:
- Investment thesis
- Industry preference
- Stage focus
- Geographical interest

### 4. Connection
Mutual opt-in connection with messaging and video call integration

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

### Frontend
- **Framework**: React 19 with TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui components
- **Charts**: Recharts for data visualization
- **Real-time**: Socket.io for live updates

### Backend
- **Framework**: NestJS (Node.js) + TypeORM
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with TimescaleDB (time-series data)
- **Authentication**: JWT + OAuth 2.0
- **Real-time**: Socket.io for messaging

### AI/ML
- **Models**: GPT-4o, Claude 3, Gemini 3 Pro
- **Vector Database**: Pinecone for semantic search
- **ML Libraries**: TensorFlow, PyTorch
- **Analysis**: Market trend analysis, competitor research

### Infrastructure
- **Deployment**: Docker + Docker Compose
- **Monitoring**: Prometheus + Grafana
- **Queue System**: BullMQ for background jobs
- **Storage**: AWS S3 for document storage
